Rules

The Rules of Spoons

  • Arrange spoons in the center and deal 4 cards to each player.
  • The dealer draws, keeps or discards, and passes one card to the left.
  • Everyone keeps passing quickly while chasing four of a kind.
  • Once a player gets four of a kind, they grab a spoon and the race begins.
  • The player left without a spoon loses a heart. Lose all 5 hearts and you are out.
  • One spoon is removed after each elimination. Last player standing wins.
